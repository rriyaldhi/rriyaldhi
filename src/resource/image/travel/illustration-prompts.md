# Travel illustration prompts

The thirteen added landmark images were generated with the built-in image generation tool, using `wall.jpg` as a style reference. China's existing `wall.jpg` and `terracotta.jpg` were retained. Generated images were exported as 960 × 640 JPEGs for the site.

## Shared direction

Single landscape 3:2 travel postcard illustration. The supplied Great Wall image is a style reference only. Match its intricate nineteenth-century black ink engraving, fine crosshatching, etched stone textures, white paper and atmospheric depth. Entirely drawn linework; pure black and white. No photography, no grayscale photo treatment, no color or sepia, no text, no border, no watermark. Keep the entire principal landmark comfortably inside frame.

- `prambanan.jpg`: Prambanan near Yogyakarta, Indonesia: a dramatic three-quarter view of the tall, intricately carved Hindu temple towers, with the main Shiva temple and smaller shrines, stone steps and a low distant landscape.
- `buddha-tooth-relic.jpg`: Buddha Tooth Relic Temple in Singapore Chinatown: its recognizable multi-storey Chinese Buddhist temple facade and tiered sweeping tiled roofs, viewed from street level with the full building visible.
- `berlin-wall.jpg`: The Berlin Wall at the preserved Bernauer Strasse memorial in Berlin: a long stretch of concrete wall panels receding diagonally, the border strip and a watchtower behind, sparse urban background. Historically recognizable architecture, no lettering or graffiti text.
- `brandenburg-gate.jpg`: Brandenburg Gate in Berlin: the complete neoclassical monument seen at a slight three-quarter angle, all six Doric columns, five passageways and the Quadriga sculpture clearly visible, broad paved foreground.
- `sydney-opera-house.jpg`: Sydney Opera House seen from across Sydney Harbour: its complete iconic overlapping sail-shaped shells on the Bennelong Point podium, fine tiled textures, engraved water ripples and distant low harbour shoreline. No Harbour Bridge needed.
- `colosseum.jpg`: The Colosseum in Rome: its complete recognizable elliptical exterior in a three-quarter view, layered arcades, broken upper outer wall and sunlit stonework, broad paved foreground. Keep the whole amphitheatre visible.

- `monte-carlo-casino.jpg`: Monte Carlo Casino in Monaco: its ornate Belle Époque main entrance facade with twin small towers, arched windows and sculpted stone decoration, viewed across the Place du Casino with a palm tree to one side. A building portrait, no logos or lettering.

- `andorra-la-vella.jpg`: Andorra la Vella in the Pyrenees: the historic stone Casa de la Vall with its little corner turret as the foreground anchor, mountain-town rooftops behind and a dramatic steep Pyrenean valley rising beyond. Recognizable Andorran town, no lettering.
- `sagrada-familia.jpg`: Antoni Gaudí's Sagrada Família in Barcelona: the richly sculpted Nativity facade and its distinctive clustered tapering spires fully visible from a three-quarter street-level view, intricate organic architectural forms and a little greenery at the edge. No scaffolding, no cranes, no lettering. Keep the spires entirely in frame.
